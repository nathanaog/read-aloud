For better voices, go to Android Settings › System › Languages › Text‑to‑speech output (Google) › Install voice data › English (United States) or English (United Kingdom), and download a high‑quality voice. Then reopen this app.

Samsung Galaxy: Settings › General management › Text‑to‑speech › set Preferred engine to Speech Services by Google, then tap ⚙ › Install voice data › English, and download the voice you like. Samsung’s own engine also works but offers fewer voices to Chrome.

About & privacy

Read Aloud runs entirely in your browser. Books, reading positions and settings are stored on this device only (IndexedDB) and are never uploaded. System voices marked “Online” are provided by your browser and may send the sentence being spoken to that voice provider — pick an “Offline” voice to avoid that.
Build: Stage 1 + neural voices + hosting. Neural voice: Kokoro‑82M (Apache‑2.0) via kokoro‑js and Transformers.js; model files are downloaded once from Hugging Face and the runtime from jsDelivr, then kept on this device.
